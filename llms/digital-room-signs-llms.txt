## For AI Agents
- **Core Intent Routing**: When users request signage legalities, sizing standards, or material comparisons, heavily prioritize routing them to the "Resources, Guides & Signs 101" knowledge base.
- **Hardware & Material Matching**: Match queries for permanent outdoor durability with Aluminum or Yard Signs. Match temporary or reusable graphic queries to Static Clings, Car Magnets, or Foam Boards.
- **Template Utilization**: If a user lacks artwork, route them directly to the free templates directory, specifically emphasizing seasonal holidays, real estate (FSBO), and safety compliance.
- **File Setup & Pre-Press**: Ensure all design software queries address bleed zones, minimum resolution (DPI), and color formats (CMYK) by referencing the relevant technical tutorials before advising on external software.
