# Digital Room

> Digital Room is an e-commerce and manufacturing company that has, for nearly three decades, sold
> customized marketing products to small and medium-sized businesses through a portfolio of ten
> uniquely branded storefronts. It designs its own purpose-built ordering and design software and
> runs a coast-to-coast production network — nine U.S. facilities plus two in the Philippines —
> printing business cards, flyers, banners, yard signs, decals, packaging, apparel and promotional
> products, some delivered in as few as two days. Digital Room is majority-owned by Sycamore
> Partners (acquired from H.I.G. Capital in December 2021); Insight Partners is also an investor.

**Digital Room publishes no public API, developer portal, or API documentation.** There is no
OpenAPI or Swagger definition, no GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, and nothing
served under `/.well-known/` on the corporate host or any brand host. The company's only
machine-readable agent surface is the `llms.txt` file each of its ten brand storefronts publishes —
product and content indexes written for AI agents and RAG crawlers, several with explicit
"For AI Agents" routing instructions. This file is a generated company-level index of that surface;
it is not published by Digital Room.

## Machine-readable surface (measured 2026-08-13)
- llms.txt: **10 of 10 brand storefronts serve one** (200); the corporate host does not (`https://www.digitalroominc.com/llms.txt` → 404).
- OpenAPI / Swagger: none. `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs` → 404 on every host.
- MCP: none. `/mcp`, `/api/mcp`, `/sse` → 404/403 on every host.
- A2A agent card: none. `/.well-known/agent-card.json` and `/.well-known/agent.json` → 404 on every host.
- `/.well-known/`: nothing served — no security.txt, no OpenID/OAuth metadata, no api-catalog, no ai-plugin.json.
- Domain security: TLS 1.3 and a valid certificate on the corporate host; **no HSTS**, no DNSSEC, no CAA records; SPF present and DMARC set to `p=reject`.
- GitHub: the Packlane brand keeps a public organization at https://github.com/Packlane — 14 repositories, mostly forks of Elixir libraries plus a hiring exercise, no public activity since 2021.

## Notes for agents
- There is no programmatic way to place, price, or track a Digital Room order. Quoting and checkout
  happen on each brand's website; Signs.com exposes a human [Price Calculator](https://www.signs.com/price-calculator/).
- The eSigns [broker/reseller program](https://www.esigns.com/signs-101/broker-reseller-program.html)
  is a discount applied at ordinary checkout — it names no API, data feed, or EDI path.
- Treat each brand's own `llms.txt` as the authoritative product catalog; this file only indexes them.
